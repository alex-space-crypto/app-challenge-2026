# Thrive JoCo 13 — Demo Video Script
## 3-Minute Congressional App Challenge Advertisement
## Post to YouTube and set to Public — judges cannot view private videos

---

## VIDEO OVERVIEW

**Goal:** Make a judge who has never seen the app understand, in 3 minutes, that this is a community thrive platform where Johnston County grows together — not just another habit tracker. The narrative flows through six pillars: community impact, thriving together, voice to change, recognizing community impacters, building a better community, and reaching goals together.

**Tone:** Confident, warm, community-focused. You're not selling features — you're showing a movement.

**Pacing:** Speak slowly. Leave pauses. Let the screen do work. Judges are often non-technical.

---

## RECORDING SETUP — DO THIS BEFORE YOU HIT RECORD

1. **Create a fresh test account** so the screen looks clean — no cluttered old goals
2. **Pre-build the Sarah scenario:** Create the "Walk the Clayton River Walk" goal with 4 verified logs already entered. When you record, you only need to add the 5th log live — the confetti, badge unlock, and animal collection popup all fire on camera
3. **Add 2-3 community posts** so the Thriving Together board isn't empty
4. **Make sure the Trick-or-Treat Main Street volunteer request is visible** with 5 volunteers already signed up so the progress bar shows 5/30 and the recent volunteers feed has entries. Click "I'll volunteer" live on camera to show it go to 6/30
5. **Pre-submit 1 community issue** (e.g. "Add sidewalks on Highway 42") with a few votes so the issue list isn't empty. Then submit a second one live on camera.
6. **Pre-submit 1-2 award nominations** so the Pending Nominations section has content. Ideally mark one as "recognized" so the recognized award recipients card is visible.
7. **Pre-log a few County Challenge contributions** so the progress bar shows real progress and the recent contributions feed has entries. Pre-submit one community challenge (e.g. "Clean up the Neuse River — 200 bags of trash") so the Submit a Challenge feature can be shown with a second live submission on camera
8. **Make sure the Updates from District 13 banner is visible** — keep it in frame during that section
9. **Close other browser tabs** — judges can see your tab bar
10. **Record at 1080p or higher** — use screen recording software like OBS, Loom, or QuickTime

## RECORDING TIPS

- **Record in one continuous take** if possible — judges prefer seeing real interaction over edited cuts
- **Speak slowly and clearly** — you know the app, they don't. Pause after key moments
- **Show real emotion when the confetti fires** — it proves the app feels rewarding, not just functional
- **When showing the Help Board, emphasize the volunteer progress bar and the recent volunteer feed** — say "five neighbors already signed up" and tap the volunteer button live to show the count and feed update
- **When showing the Community Champions section, highlight the blue styling and the broad nomination language** — say "it doesn't have to be a neighbor — it can be a police officer, a teacher, a cashier, anyone"
- **When you tap 'Submit an Issue', slow down** — let judges see the form, the category dropdown, and the glowing button. This is a standout feature.
- **Keep it under 3 minutes** — the CAC strictly enforces the 1-3 minute limit. Aim for 2:55
- **Do a dry run first** — practice the full script with the app open before recording the real take
- **Upload to YouTube and set to Public** — judges cannot view private or unlisted videos. Test the link in an incognito window to confirm it's accessible
