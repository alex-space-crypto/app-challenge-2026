# Thrive JoCo 13 — Congressional App Challenge Demo Video Script
## (1–3 minutes, post to YouTube as Public)

---

### INTRO (0:00 – 0:20)

**[Screen: Login screen, then app opens to Community tab]**

"Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], and this is Thrive JoCo 13 — a habit-tracking and community wellness app built for the people of Johnston County, North Carolina, right here in District 13.

Most fitness apps are just private checklists. Nothing connects personal health to the real places and people around you. We built something different."

---

### COMMUNITY IMPACT (0:20 – 0:55)

**[Screen: Community tab — scroll to show the Johnston County Community Impact panel with the 2,300+ verified activities, 180+ goals completed, 400+ community service logs]**

"When you open the app, the first thing you see isn't your own goals — it's our community. This is the Johnston County Community Impact dashboard. It shows the combined effort of everyone in the county — over 2,300 verified activities logged, 180 goals completed, and 400 community service logs from neighbors helping neighbors."

**[Screen: Scroll down to show the Thriving Together banner, help board, and community resources]**

"Below that is the community board where neighbors share wins and encourage each other, a volunteer help board where residents can request assistance and others can sign up to help, and links to real local resources — the food bank, the libraries, the senior center."

---

### GOALS & VERIFICATION (0:55 – 1:50)

**[Screen: Tap the Goals tab. Show creating a goal from the Fitness pathway]**

"Now let's set a personal goal. I pick the Fitness pathway and choose 'Walk the Clayton River Walk,' set a target of 5 walks, and it's pinned to its real location on the map."

**[Screen: Tap 'Log & Verify' on the goal. Fill in date, time, verifier name 'Marcus', location, note]**

"Here's what makes our app different — verification. After each walk, I tap Log and Verify. I enter the date, the time, who confirmed I did it — my friend Marcus — and where it happened. Only verified logs count toward my streak, my level, and my badges. No self-reported checkmarks. Progress is honest."

**[Screen: Show the 5th log — confetti fires, badge unlocks, reward popup appears]**

"On my 5th verified walk, I hit my goal. Confetti fires. I unlock the Trailblazer Pin badge and earn a collectible animal — a Neuse River otter. Every verified log also gives you a chance to collect a local animal for your collection."

---

### PATHWAYS & BADGES (1:50 – 2:15)

**[Screen: Tap Pathways tab — show the 7 pathways with Johnston County-specific suggestions]**

"We built seven pathways — Sustainability, Reading, Fitness, Hydration, Community Service, Chores, and Local Food — each with goal suggestions tied to real Johnston County places like the Neuse River Greenway, Howell Woods, and the Smithfield Farmers Market."

**[Screen: Tap Badges tab — show the 4-tier badge grid and tiered progression]**

"Badges come in four tiers — bronze, silver, gold, and platinum — with rewards specific to each category. You earn them by actually doing the work, not just by creating them."

---

### MAP (2:15 – 2:40)

**[Screen: Tap Map tab — show the interactive Leaflet map of Johnston County with goal pins. Tap a pin to show details]**

"The Map tab shows every public goal as a pin across Johnston County. Tap any pin to see the goal, its address, and your progress. Goals at private locations like your home are excluded entirely — your address never appears on the shared map, but the goal still tracks normally."

**[Screen: Return to Community tab, show the header with 'Thrive JoCo 13' linking to knott.house.gov]**

"Thrive JoCo 13 was built with React, TypeScript, Firebase Authentication, and Supabase. It turns wellness into a shared, local, verified experience — because health isn't just about tracking. It's about trust, community, and the places we call home.

District 13, rise together."

## Recording Tips

1. **Use a fresh account** so badges and goals look clean and uncluttered
2. **Pre-create the "Sarah" scenario** — the Walk the Clayton River Walk goal with 4 logs already done, so you only need to add the 5th live to trigger confetti
3. **Record in one take if possible** — judges prefer seeing real interaction over edited cuts
4. **Speak slowly and clearly** — judges are often non-technical community members
5. **Show real emotion when the confetti fires** — it demonstrates that the app feels rewarding
6. **Keep it under 3 minutes** — the CAC strictly enforces the 1-3 minute limit
7. **Upload to YouTube and set to Public** — judges cannot view private or unlisted videos
